Song Minted AI Services

How the app actually produces a downloadable, user-owned song. Provider selection, system architecture, generation pipeline, credits, licensing, and cost — the build brief for your developer.

00 · Guiding Principle

Don't train a model. Don't call vendors from the phone. Building a music model costs millions and years. Instead, integrate licensed third-party APIs behind a thin backend of your own that holds the keys, meters credits, and stores the finished audio. This is how every App Store music app in your screenshots is built.

Recommended v1 stack

  • ◆Mureka for the "Mint Song" flow — affordable, licensed, royalty-free.
  • ◆ElevenLabs for "Sing in your own voice" (cloning) and covers.
  • ◆Later: add a premium "Ultra" tier powered by ElevenLabs Music for audiophile output.

Why not the cheapest?

There is no official Suno API. The wrappers can break without notice and disclaim all warranties — a real liability once you're charging users and Apple is reviewing your app. Quality between licensed options is now close enough that your brand, voice cloning, and Explore feed — not raw audio — are the differentiator.

01b · The EvoLink Angle

EvoLink (evolink.ai) is an aggregator gateway: one API key routes to Suno v4/v4.5/v5 plus image, video, and LLM models. It's the polished version of the "Suno wrapper" path — cheap, top audio quality, and it maps cleanly onto this architecture (async submit → poll /v1/tasks/{id} or webhook, 72-hour audio URLs, Personas incl. a V5 voice_persona).

Why it's tempting

  • ✓Best quality, ~12¢/song (2 tracks per call) — Suno v5 is the "wow" audio.
  • ✓One key powers the whole app: songs (Suno), cover art (image models), music-video-from-photo (OmniHuman/Veo/Sora), lyric writing (LLM).
  • ✓Integration matches the prototype's async progress screen exactly.

The catch

  • !Unofficial Suno access — same ToS gray area; their FAQ says "review upstream provider requirements."
  • !No written commercial-rights guarantee like Mureka/ElevenLabs give.
  • !Beta models "may experience occasional downtime."
Verdict: Use EvoLink to build and launch the MVP fast and cheap — and for the free tier/demos. Keep the backend provider-agnostic so paid users (where you promise ownership + commercial rights) can be routed to Mureka or ElevenLabs. Same app, swap the engine.

Design implication: define one internal generateSong(params) interface in the backend with pluggable adapters — EvoLinkAdapter, MurekaAdapter, ElevenLabsAdapter — chosen per request by the user's tier. The app never knows or cares which engine ran.

The golden rule: API keys live only on the backend. If they ship in the app, anyone can extract them and burn your credits.

🎛️ Backend

Auth, user records, credit ledger, job queue, provider calls, webhooks. Supabase or Firebase covers auth + DB + storage + serverless functions with minimal code.

🗄️ Storage

Finished MP3/WAV in Cloudflare R2 or AWS S3. Serve via short-lived signed URLs so only the owner can download.

💳 Payments

Apple In-App Purchase for subscriptions, managed with RevenueCat. Backend trusts RevenueCat's entitlement to unlock credits.

03 · The "Mint Song" Pipeline
1
App submits. Sends prompt/lyrics + style + genre + mood + voice to POST /mint with the user's auth token.
2
Backend checks credits. If the user has ≥1 credit, it deducts one and creates a job record with status queued. If not → returns "paywall."
3
Backend calls the provider (Mureka) with your server-side key, gets a provider job id, stores it, sets status generating.
4
App polls GET /job/{id} every ~2s (this is exactly the progress screen the prototype already mocks), or the backend receives a provider webhook.
5
On completion the backend downloads the audio, stores it in R2/S3, writes the song to the user's Vault, and returns a signed download URL.
6
App plays & offers download. On failure, the backend refunds the credit automatically.
04 · Voice Cloning & Covers

Enrolment (once)

  • 1User records a short voice sample in-app.
  • 2Backend sends it to ElevenLabs → gets a private voice_id stored against the user.
  • 3Require an explicit consent checkbox ("this is my own voice") — legal + Apple requirement.

Covers

For "sing any song," the user's voice_id is applied to a chosen track. Only allow the user's own voice or your licensed AI personas (Nova, Blaze, Onyx…). Never offer real named celebrities — that's the lawsuit vector. Keep personas clearly fictional.

05 · Credits & Monetisation

1 credit = 1 mint. New users get a few free credits (feel the magic); credits refill with a Pro subscription or one-time packs.

  • ◉Free: 3 starter credits, watermarked or standard quality.
  • ◉Pro (Weekly €5.99 · Yearly €49.99 · Lifetime €79.99): unlimited/large monthly credit bucket, premium voices, HD + commercial license.
  • ◉Packs: one-time IAP (e.g. €7.99 premium-voice bundle) for non-subscribers.
Protect your margin: even "unlimited" plans need a soft fair-use cap (e.g. a generous daily credit ceiling) so a handful of power users can't run your provider bill past their subscription.

A Yearly Pro user would need to mint hundreds of songs before eroding margin on Mureka, and dozens on ElevenLabs Music — hence the fair-use cap. Add fixed monthly floors: Supabase/Firebase (free→~$25/mo early), R2/S3 storage (pennies/GB), RevenueCat (free under ~$2.5k MTR). Early monthly infra ≈ $30–80 before scale.

07 · Legal & Compliance Checklist

Do

  • ✓Use officially-licensed providers; pass their commercial rights to paying users in your Terms.
  • ✓Require voice-consent confirmation before cloning.
  • ✓Add content moderation on prompts/lyrics (hate, explicit, minors).
  • ✓Publish clear Terms, Privacy, and a Licensing page (already linked on the landing page).

Don't

  • ✕Offer real named-celebrity voices or "in the style of [artist]" as a selling point.
  • ✕Ship provider API keys inside the app binary.
  • ✕Rely on unofficial Suno wrappers for production.
  • ✕Let users clone a voice that isn't theirs without permission.
08 · Build Roadmap
Phase 1
Backend spine. Supabase project: auth, users, credit ledger, /mint + /job functions, R2 storage, and a pluggable generateSong() adapter. Wire the EvoLink adapter first (fastest + cheapest) to ship a working end-to-end song from the existing prototype UI; keep Mureka/ElevenLabs adapters behind the same interface.
Phase 2
Monetisation. Apple IAP via RevenueCat, paywall, credit refills, free-tier caps.
Phase 3
Voice + covers. ElevenLabs enrolment, consent, cover flow with licensed personas.
Phase 4
Community + premium. Explore feed publishing, likes/plays, and the ElevenLabs-powered "Ultra" quality tier.
