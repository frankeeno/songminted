01 · What We're Building
Song Minted turns anyone into a hit-maker in seconds — describe a feeling, paste a lyric, or drop a photo, and mint a full, radio-ready song in your own voice.

The objective: become the friendliest, most shareable AI music studio on mobile. Suno and Udio won the "pro creator" web audience. Mozart and the App Store clones won impulse consumers with gifting and celebrity-voice hooks. Song Minted's opening is the gap between them — the polish and community of the pro tools, delivered with the one-tap, emotional, giftable simplicity that actually converts on mobile.

02 · Who It's For

🎁 The Gifter

Wants a personal song for a birthday, anniversary, or wedding. Non-musical, emotionally motivated, willing to pay for something meaningful. Highest conversion.

📱 The Creator

Makes TikToks / Reels and needs original, copyright-safe tracks and covers to stand out. Values speed, trends, and shareability.

🎧 The Dabbler

Curious, plays with prompts for fun, sings covers in celebrity-style voices. Drives virality and word-of-mouth via the Explore feed.

03 · What We're Selling

The promise

We don't sell "AI music generation." We sell the feeling of having made something — a song that's yours, ready to gift, post, or replay. Emotion first, technology second.

🎵 Full songs with vocals🎤 Your own voice, cloned 🎭 20+ styles & moods🎬 Music videos from a photo 🚀 Publish to Explore⬇ HD download + license
